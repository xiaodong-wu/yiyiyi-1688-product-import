Company Introduction

Yiant Technologies is a Guangzhou-based supplier serving battery pack manufacturers, electrical equipment companies and industrial customers worldwide. Our portfolio covers insulation paper, adhesive tapes, EVA foam, Mylar, nickel strips, connectors, battery cases, battery indicators and epoxy fiberglass laminates.

Supported by specialized production and converting resources, we provide material selection, die cutting, slitting, CNC machining, sampling and coordinated supply services. Our goal is to help customers simplify sourcing while improving product consistency and project efficiency.
